Since childhood, I was always good in drawing — characters, places, anything creative. But it was in college when I actually started thinking how things work and why they are made that way. This habit started from games, where I always got irritated with how the app or interface looks and works, and I kept thinking how it should be instead. This is where my journey into UX/UI actually began — much before I even knew this field exists. I started as a graphic designer, and slowly this curiosity turned into passion for creating designs that solve real problem and also evoke emotion and tell story.
Along this journey, I also worked on branding and rebranding project where I aligned 6 group companies under one identity, similar to how Bajaj Group Group have many sub companies working under one group image. Now I work with good clients and companies who trust me enough to give access and responsibility to solve their problems, which pushed me more into research driven, human-centered design thinking.
Outside work, I love riding bikes and cars, and I always end up doing small adjustments to make them better — this is basically UX thinking in everyday life for me. I also enjoy painting, drawing, knitting, sewing and anything creative that challenge me. And now, with AI as part of my process, I am able to work more effectively — spending more time understanding people and building better solutions. Let's connect!
